# Safety and publishing

## Finance claims

- Do not promise profit, guaranteed win rate, or loss-free outcomes.
- Do not fabricate prices, dates, market events, or performance data.
- Use conditional language for technical patterns and market scenarios.
- Add an educational-information disclaimer when appropriate.

## Identity and copyright

- Do not copy competitor scripts, watermarks, proprietary charts, or likenesses.
- Record origin and license for downloaded media.
- Use official or licensed real-world media for people, institutions, and current events.

## External actions

- HeyGen render requires confirmation because it can consume credit.
- Publishing requires confirmation of platform, profile, final file, and schedule.
- Facebook and YouTube use their dedicated project publishers. TikTok may fall back to a manual package.
- Verify platform response and save `publish.json`; do not treat a successful command exit alone as proof that the public post is correct.
